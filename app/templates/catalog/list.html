How this works
  1. Your catalog is the price list quotes are built from: each item has a cost price (what you pay) and a sell price (what you charge).
  2. Add items one at a time with Add item, or paste a whole vendor list into ✨ Bulk import.
  3. Use custom fields on an item for anything specific to your industry (resolution, PoE class, pipe diameter…).
  4. Deleting an item hides it from new quotes — existing quotes keep their saved prices and descriptions.

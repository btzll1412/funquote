How this works
  1. Set your organization's display name here.
  2. Use the AI assist toggle to turn ✨ features on or off for everyone in your organization. Off means FunQuote is a fully manual quoting tool.
  3. To use AI assist, also connect your own AI account or server under AI provider.

With AI assist off, the system works as a fully manual quoting tool. AI never runs on its own either way — it is only invoked when a user explicitly asks, and every call is recorded in the AI task log.

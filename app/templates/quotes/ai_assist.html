How this works
  1. Paste what the customer asked for — an email, a text message, or notes from a call.
  2. Optionally pick which customer this is for.
  3. Click Draft quote with AI. Your organization's own AI reads the request and matches items to your catalog.
  4. You land in the quote builder with a draft: matched items are priced from your catalog (the AI never sets prices), and anything it couldn't match is highlighted for you to resolve.
  5. Review, edit, then save — nothing is stored until you do.
